## CupaGoovno v1.2 changelog

### Fixes
- Fixed a bug where Carrots sometimes stopped firing.
- Fixed a bug where Potato's parryable projectiles didn't correctly count parries in the final screen.
- Fixed Parry Sugar sometimes causing parry effect spam and lags.

### Rebalance
- Genie's third phase - Made ghosts at the top and bottom invincible.
- Genie's final phase - Turned the circle around which pyramids spin into a wider elipse to give the player more space and also make it harder to spam Whetstone.
- Matchstick's (Dragon) final phase - Slightly decreased fire rate.
- Goopy's (Slime) final phase - Made it harder.
- Werner's (Mouse) first phase - Reduced boss movement to give the player more space.

### Other
- Added a compatiblity warning when using other mods.
- Added a feedback notification.
- Added a notification explaining how to equip new charm and weapon.
- Updated Wally's (Bird) warning to a notification box used by the other notifications. Also now it appears only once per session.
