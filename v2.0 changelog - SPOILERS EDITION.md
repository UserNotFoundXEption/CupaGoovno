## CupaGoovno v2.0 changelog - SPOILERS EDITION

### New boss - Moai
- 10 attacks, combined in 25 different pairs.
- Each attack has its parameters. (e.g. sometimes projectiles can be bigger but less numerous).
- Enhanced with machine learning algorythms, seeking the best pairs and parameters to beat your ass.
- Talk to a new NPC on Inwel Isle I to fight him.
- Has ULTRAMOAI variant with 10 times more health and sounds from ULTRAKILL.
- Documentation: https://github.com/UserNotFoundXEption/CupaGoovno/blob/main/Moai%20documentation/README.md

### New weapons
- Mangetsunami
    - Basic:
        - Charged. Fire rate same as Charger.
        - Uncharged:
            - Damage: 5
            - Projectiles: 1
        - Charged:
            - Bounces off screen, increasing its damage.
            - Bounces: 2
            - Damage multiplier per bounce: 1.5
            - Damage: 15
            - Projectiles: 3
    - Ex:
        - Dash into Ex to boost its speed and damage.
        - Damage: 25
        - Damage after dash: 50
- Peeshooter
    - Basic:
        - Deals low damage but fills your supermeter much faster.
        - Damage: 2
        - Supermeter multiplier: 5
    - Ex:
        - Spawns a puddle, doubling your supermeter gain for the other weapon as long as you're standing in it.
        - Duration: 15 seconds

### New Super Arts
- Sandevistan
    - Slows down everything except you by 70%.
    - Dealing damage depletes supermeter faster.
    - Max duration: 25 seconds.
    - Max duration in run'n'guns: 5 seconds.
    - Max damage: 150.
- Boo fist
    - Summons a giant ghost fist that will destroy up to 10 enemy projectiles.

### New charms
- Balanced Blade
    - Dealing damage decreases current weapon's damage but boosts the other one.
    - Passive damage boost: 20%.
- Milk Carton
    - Parry to receive milk charge. Use Ex to fire a bonus projectile.
    - Damage: 50.
- Gravity Globe
    - Press R on keybord or R3 on most controllers to reverse gravity.

### New achievements
- Each achievement unlocks a new item. (You wanted them for free, didn't you?)
- Using Casual Mode, Practise Mode and other mods disables achievements.
- You can only unlock them in non-DLC boss fights.
- King Dice's mini-bosses don't count.
- Achievements list:
    - Keep Super Art IV (Berserker) active for 20 seconds without getting hit.
    - Defeat a boss using Crackshot and Striker.
    - Defeat a boss using Chaos Relic.
    - Defeat a boss while under influence of Super Art II. (Can't shoot since v1.3).
    - Survive Dancer's Rift with at least rank B.
    - Defeat Wally Warbles without parrying.
    - Defeat King Dice with 9 health remaining.

### New minigame
- Music minigame inspired by Rift of the Necrodancer.
- Parry one of 3 string at the right moment to hit an enemy. Try to land combos of perfect hits and earn a high score!
- Each enemy has different behaviour. Some can move between columns, some need to be hit multiple times. Learn their patterns and don't get confused!
- Talk to a new NPC on Inkwel Isle I to play it.
- Can't play as Chalice.

### New challenge in run'n'gun and mausoleum levels
- Angry Mugman will follow you by repeating all your moves with a delay.
- In run'n'gun levels, the delay starts at 1 seconds (1.5 for Rugged Ridge) and decreases by 0.01 second with every second.
- In mausoleum levels, the delay is always 1 second and Mugman instantly kills you on hit.

### Modded Angel and Devil secret boss
- Fires waves of bullets from left, top and right. Half of bullets are from the devil, the others from the angel.
- Turning around summons a new wave.
- Summons a new wave if there's been 2 seconds without any new wave.
