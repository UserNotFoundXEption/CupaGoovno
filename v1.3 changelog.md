## CupaGoovno v1.3 changelog

### New content
- Super Art 4 - Berserker. Allows oyu to fire both weapons with fire rate of the currently used one but it also speeds up time and descreases your damage. Are you fast enough?
    - Check out equipment changelog to learn more: https://github.com/UserNotFoundXEption/CupaGoovno/blob/main/equipment%20changelog.md
- Replaced Upshot rework with a new weapon - Skytickler. Upshot now works as it originally used to. Skytickler works like Upshot but projectiles more upwards.

### Fixes
- Fixed a bug where Carrots sometimes stopped firing. (this time for real)
- Fixed notifications sometimes appearing in the wrong place.
- Fixed custom weapon projectiles sometimes staying in the air.
- Added grey icons for custom items so it doesn't bug and show another weapon.
- Fixed Short Frog sometimes rolling too fast while transitioning to phase 3.
- Fixed Flower's visual effect disappearing after getting hit by phase 2 pollen.

### Rebalance
- Goopy's (Slime) phase 1 and 2 - Made him much more predictable in both phases and slower in phase 2.
- Made Short Frog stop clapping immediately when health is low enough to transition to phase 3.
- Djimmi's phase 4 - Made the main bullet hell less random.
- Sally's phase 1 - Bottle spreadshot attack now also fires bottles on the sides of the arena to prevent players from cheesing and standing in 1 place.
- Fury Heart - Doubled the damage increase speed.
- Super 2 - Lasts shorter, uses only 3 cards (you still have to fully fill supermeter) and you can't shoot while it lasts.

### Other
- Added new options in pause menu allowing you to hide notifications or damage multiplier counter and disable falling through platforms while parrying. (Some levels have it disabled by default)
