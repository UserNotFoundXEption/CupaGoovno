## CupaGoovno v2.0 changelog - NO SPOILERS EDITION

### New content
- New boss enhanced with machine learning.
    - Talk to a new NPC on Inwel Isle I to fight him.
    - Documentation: https://github.com/UserNotFoundXEption/CupaGoovno/blob/main/Moai%20documentation/README.md
- 2 new weapons.
- 2 new Super Arts.
- 3 new charms.
- 7 new achievements.
    - Each achievement unlocks a new item. (You wanted them for free, didn't you?)
    - Using Casual Mode, Practise Mode and other mods disables achievements.
    - You can only unlock them in non-DLC boss fights.
    - King Dice's mini-bosses don't count.
- New special something.
    - Talk to a new NPC on Inwel Isle I to find it.
- New challenge in run'n'gun and mausoleum levels.
- Modded Angel and Devil secret boss.

### Reworks and rebalances
- Weapons
    - Homing weapons buff
        - Chaser basic and plane bomb Ex projectiles follow a cursor you can move while holding Lock/Aim key.
        - Chaser damage: 1.5 ➔ 2.
        - Plane bomb Ex damage: 4 ➔ 5.
        - Chaser Ex projectiles rotate in wider radius: 100 ➔ 250.
    - Crackshot Ex doesn't break on collission with enemy.
    - Converge opening time: 1 ➔ 20.
    - Striker
        - Ex damage: 12 ➔ 15.
        - Ex max damage: 35 ➔ 50.
    - Charger doesn't stop charging after dash.
    - Plane Super Art deals damage instantly instead of dealing it 4 times every 0.25 second. (May cause issues with high damage multipliers)
- Charms
    - Parry Sugar auto-parry continues after using Ex mid-air.
    - Heart ring damage reduction per parry: 5% ➔ 3%. 
    - Fury Heart
        - Prevents healing.
        - Bonus damage persists between King Dice mini-boss battles but doesn't increase in the main area.
- Other
    - Lock/Aim key stops your movement in plane levels.
