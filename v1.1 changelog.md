## CupaGoovno v1.1 changelog

### Bosses
- Increased Goopy's (Slime) jumping cooldowns.
- Made Djimmi's (Genie) last phase rings disappear after parrying.
- Fixed Cagney's (Flower) visual effect sometimes not disappearing after the fight.
- Fixed Sally sometimes giving up on fight in phase 1.
- Made Frogs' tiger attack in phase 3 a bit more lethal.
- Reworked Root Pack (Veggies) phase 3 attack cooldown mechanics so it's more consistent.
- Made horseshoes in Beppi (Clown) phase 1 less lethal.
- Added more variety to Dr. Kahl's Robot "Space Invaders" phase.
    - 4 rows of regular bots.
    - 1 row of bots firing larger but parryable projectiles.
    - 1 row of bots firing a stream of projectiles aiming at the player.

### Equipment
- Fixed the mod breaking sprites of other custom equipment mods. Still might not be fully compatible with them. Use at your own risk.
- Restored original Peashooter properties for "Peashooter only" players. Instead, added a new weapon - Striker. Acts like Peashooter but with lower Basic damage and higher Ex damage. Press "down" twice in the weapon menu to find it.

### Localization
- Made some custom descriptions more clear.
- Better Russian translations.
